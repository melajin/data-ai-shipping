## Input and rules (sequence_rules.py, Luna owner)

Canonical input frame: rfid_id string, ts integer hours, source_ordinal, len_m,bbox_m,cen_lon,cen_lat,score. Exactly unique RFID/ts, numeric valid coordinates optional. Use Round2 execution_002/featured_rows.parquet selected columns, not old U/C in rules. Gear registry from Round3: rfid_id,primary_gear,is_single_code. Unknown/mixed labels remain unknown.

Geometry valid = finite len/bbox and >=0. Small = valid and len<=100 AND bbox<=100 (fixed design assumption, not physical stopping). Stage0_ok=current geometry valid and finite legacy score.
Stage1_ok=stage0 AND exact observed t-1 geometry valid and NOT small AND current small. No gap interpolation.
Stage2_ok=stage1 AND exact observed t+1 geometry valid and small. Candidate timestamp=t. Missing/invalid adjacent observations are deferred flags, not proof of no behavior. Stage2 is the common S candidate pool.

For every candidate t, own past baseline uses only geometry-valid consecutive triples centered at s where s-1,s,s+1 all exist, s-1>=t-720 and s+1<t. Transition numerator=NOT small(s-1) AND small(s) AND small(s+1). Denominator=all valid consecutive triples in that window. Eligible if >=24 triples and >=3 distinct center dates (s//24). Rate=numerator/denominator. For each OTHER same-single-code RFID compute its own past rate at the SAME t and same eligibility. Need >=5 eligible peers. Own eligibility and label required. Peer percentile p=(#peer_rates < own_rate +.5*#peer_rates==own_rate)/npeers. Gear priority=1-p. No accident labels/coords or future triple in baseline. Combined jigging/longline singleton has no peer rule.

Stage3_ok=stage2 and own/peer/gear eligibility. Ledger records self counts/rate, eligible-peer count, percentile, gear priority and missing reasons. Stage3 means eligible for gear comparison, not confirmed anomaly. Other stage2 candidates are deferred in gear method rather than called normal.

Method pools per RFID: B0=all stage0; S=stage2; G=stage3 if nonempty, else stage2 if nonempty, else stage0. S falls back to stage0 only if no stage2. G fallback reason must specify no gear-eligible candidates vs no common transitions. All methods share cap=min(4,len(actual G pool)); no observations =>cap0. S and B0 retain their own pools but emit at most cap. No duplicate ts, no additional spacing. Top1=rank1 of same list.
B0/S (including fallbacks) rank legacy score descending, ts ascending,ordinal ascending. G with actual stage3 ranks gear priority descending, then legacy score descending,ts,ordinal; otherwise common/base score ranking. predictions store method_score actually used, legacy_score separately, score_kind,method,rank,ts,ordinal,actual_pool_n,cap,fallback_reason. No accident probabilities.

API build_rules(frame,gear_registry)->dict with ledger DataFrame, predictions DataFrame, pool_manifest DataFrame, diagnostics dict. pool_manifest has one row per RFID/method with columns rfid_id,method,pool_stage (stage0/stage2/stage3),actual_pool_n,cap,fallback_reason. Evaluator obtains the actual pool from ledger RFID and the corresponding boolean stage mask; no independent guess about fallback. Ledger mask column names are stage0_ok,stage1_ok,stage2_ok,stage3_ok; ts/rfid_id/source_ordinal stay unchanged. Keep ledger original row/index order. Include small, previous/next observed validity and gap reasons, lineage, gear history and eligibility. For explanation only add t+2 status (missing/invalid/still_small/condition_exit) and past30day same .05degree-cell fraction (valid past positions only; no harbor names). These explanations do not affect ranking. Preserve missing/left/right censoring flags.
Ownership: sequence_rules.py,check_sequence_rules.py,rule_checks.json,RULE_NOTES.md. Small meaningful fixtures: common onset/persistence, broken gaps, no current/future baseline, three-day/24-triple/5-peer boundaries, future append invariance of existing scoring fields (t+1 is intentionally used for persistence), shared caps and different G/S/B0 winners, singleton gear/no-history fallback.
