At current shape x, p=(#past<x+.5#past==x)/n; self_rarity=1-2min(p,1-p). Peer rarity compute same statistic per eligible vessel at x, then mean with equal vessel weight. G uses self_rarity FIRST, peer_rarity SECOND, legacy THIRD,ts asc,ordinal asc. S uses self_rarity,legacy,ts,ordinal.
